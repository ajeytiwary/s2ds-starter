Dataset Title: Data for: Relationship between hydrological restoration and the recovery of vegetation communities in boreal forestry-drained peatlands
Principal Investigator: Merja Elo

File Name : site_data.csv
Attributes: Information of the 40 study sites. Please see the full description of the data in Elo et al. Relationship between hydrological restoration and the recovery of vegetation communities in boreal forestry-drained peatlands.
ID = Unique identification number for each site
N = N-coordinate (~WGS84) for each site; Coordinate Reference System:EPSG:4258
E = E-coordinate (~WGS84) for each site; Coordinate Reference System:EPSG:4258
Type = Ecosystem type (spruce mire forest poor / spruce mire forest rich / pine mire forest poor/ pine mire forest rich / open mire poor / open mire rich)
Treatment = restored (drained for foresty in ~1960-1970 and restored during the project) / pristine (no drainage for forestry or restoration)
Sampling_0 = The year for vegetation sampling before restoration; first sampling in pristine sites
Sampling_2 = The year for vegetation sampling two years after restoration in restored sites; second sampling in pristine sites
Sampling_5 = The year for vegetation sampling five years after restoration in restored sites; third sampling in pristine sites
Sampling_10 = The year for vegetation sampling ten years after restoration in restored sites; fourth sampling in pristine sites

File Name: species_groups_data.csv
Attributes: Average cover percentage (from 10 1-m2 plots on each site) for different species groups for each site and sampling time. Please see the full description of the definition of the groups in Elo et al.  Relationship between hydrological restoration and the recovery of vegetation communities in boreal forestry-drained peatlands
ID = Unique identification number for each site
Time = Sampling (0 = before restoration/first sampling in pristine sites, 2 = two years after restoration/second sampling in pristine sites, 5 = five years after restoration/third sampling in pristine sites, 10 = 10 years after restoration in restored sites/fourth sampling in pristine sites)
Pristine = Average cover percentage of all vascular plant and moss species that are more abundant in pristine than in in drained and restored sites before restoration
None = Average cover percentage of all vascular plant and moss species that do not differ in their abundance in pristine and in drained/restored sites before restoration
Drainage = Average cover percentage of all vascular plant and moss species that are less abundant in pristine than in drained and restored sites before restoration
Moss_hummock = Average cover percentage of moss species (other than Sphagna) that occur only on hummock
Moss_lawn = Average cover percentage of moss species (other than Sphagna) that occur on lawn or on hummock and lawn
Moss_hollow = Average cover percentage of moss species (other than Sphagna) that occur on hollow or on lawn and hollow
Sphagnum_hummock = Average cover percentage of Sphagnum species that occur only on hummock
Sphagnum_lawn = Average cover percentage of Sphagnum species that occur on lawn or on hummock and lawn
Sphagnum_hollow = Average cover percentage of Sphagnum species that occur on hollow or on lawn and hollow
Sphagnum_mixed = Average cover percentage of Sphagnum species that can not be reliable separated

File Name: hydrological_data.csv
Attributes: Median/mean values for the measures hydrological characteristics for each site and sampling time. Please see the full description in Elo et al.  Relationship between hydrological restoration and the recovery of vegetation communities in boreal forestry-drained peatlands
ID = Unique identification number for each site
Time = Sampling (0 = before restoration/first sampling in pristine sites, 2 = two years after restoration/second sampling in pristine sites, 5 = five years after restoration/third sampling in pristine sites, 10 = 10 years after restoration in restored sites/fourth sampling in pristine sites)
WT_ES = Early summer median water table level (cm; 0 at peatland surface, < 0 below peatland surface)
WT_MS = Mid-summer median water table level (cm; 0 at peatland surface, < 0 below peatland surface)
pH = Mean pH
EC = Mean electrical conductivity (mSm-1) 
ABS = Mean UV absorbance at 254 nm (cm-1) 
N = Mean total soluble nitrogen (µgl-1)
P = Mean total soluble phosphorus (µgl-1) 
DOC = Mean dissolved organic carbon (mgl-1)
